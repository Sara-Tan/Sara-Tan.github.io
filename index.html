AI Engineer
Huawei R&D UK LLM Reasoning Multi-Agent Systems AI for Science Biomedical Science PhD

I’m an AI Engineer at Huawei Technologies R&D UK (London), where I research inference-time LLM reasoning and build agentic systems for research intelligence and 3D scene reconstruction.

Before moving into AI, I spent seven years in life sciences research. I hold a PhD in Biomedical Science from the University of Sheffield, where I also worked as a Postdoctoral Research Fellow on genetics, mechanobiology, and biophysics, leading projects funded by the Marie Skłodowska-Curie Actions and the Wellcome Trust. I have published first-author papers in Nature Communications and Development, and built six tools to automate life sciences workflows, including the open-source QuantifyPolarity (200+ downloads).

I have reviewed for Development, Cell Reports, NeurIPS workshops, and ICLR.
