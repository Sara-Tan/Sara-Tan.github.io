I am an AI Engineer specialising in LLM reasoning and multi-agent systems, with an MSc and PhD in Biomedical Engineering.

At Huawei Technologies R&D UK, I research inference-time LLM reasoning and build agentic systems for research intelligence and 3D scene reconstruction. My first-author work was accepted at the NeurIPS TTCL 2026 workshop.

Before moving into AI, I developed six image-analysis and machine-learning software tools, including the open-source _QuantifyPolarity_ (200+ downloads).

I have also authored several peer-reviewed papers and reviewed for ICLR, NeurIPS workshops, _Development_, and _Cell Reports_.
