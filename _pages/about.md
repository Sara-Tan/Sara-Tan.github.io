I'm an **AI Engineer** at **Huawei Technologies R&D UK** (London), where I research **inference-time LLM reasoning** and build **agentic systems** for research intelligence and 3D scene reconstruction.

Before moving into AI, I spent seven years in life sciences research. I hold a PhD in Biomedical Science from the University of Sheffield, where I also worked as a Postdoctoral Research Fellow on genetics, mechanobiology, and biophysics, leading projects funded by the Marie Skłodowska-Curie Actions and the Wellcome Trust. I have published first-author papers in _Nature Communications_ and _Development_, and built six tools to automate life sciences workflows, including the open-source _QuantifyPolarity_ (200+ downloads).

I have reviewed for _Development_, _Cell Reports_, NeurIPS workshops, and ICLR.
